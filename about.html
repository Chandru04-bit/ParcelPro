From a Single Van to a Global Logistics Platform

ParcelPro was founded in 2018 with a simple but ambitious vision: to make parcel delivery fast, transparent, and truly reliable for everyone. What started as a single delivery van and three passionate co-founders in a rented warehouse has grown into a technology-driven logistics powerhouse serving 220+ countries.

We invested early in proprietary route-optimization algorithms, real-time tracking, and a customer-first platform that empowers both senders and recipients. ParcelPro launches in a 3,000 sq. ft. warehouse with 12 team members and one city-wide delivery route in New York. First customers sign up within 48 hours.
