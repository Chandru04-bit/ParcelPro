How It Works

Simple 6-Step Delivery Process

From the moment you book to the second your parcel is signed for, every step is transparent and tracked.

1

Request Pickup

Book online or via the app in under 60 seconds. Enter pickup/delivery addresses, choose a service tier, and select a convenient pickup window.

2

Package Collected

Our uniformed courier arrives at your doorstep at the scheduled time, scans the parcel, and provides a digital receipt on collection.

3

Sorting & Processing

Your parcel is sent to the nearest sorting hub where it's scanned, weighed, dimension-verified, and routed to the correct destination lane.

4

In Transit

The package travels via our optimized ground or air network. Every checkpoint is scanned and visible on your real-time tracking page.

5

Out for Delivery

Shipment reaches the destination city and is loaded onto the local delivery route. You'll get an ETA window and the courier's contact info.

6

Delivered

Your parcel is delivered to the recipient's doorstep. You receive instant confirmation, including signature or photo proof of delivery.
